## Rights review

The published terms distinguish individual internal professional use from reuse of the provider's database. They prohibit systematic extraction of a substantial part of the database for publication, resale, or creation of a competing catalogue without a separate agreement. The terms also describe access quotas and account/subscription conditions for full documents.

VAG TECH PRO is an independently distributed vehicle-repair catalogue. The current terms therefore do not establish permission for:
- bulk or systematic extraction;
- import into a distributable SQLite database;
- offline caching inside the Windows installer;
- third-party display or redistribution;
- creation of a competing or derivative catalogue.

The operator's terms are not a data licence granting VAG TECH PRO those rights. Individual professional use does not grant rights to redistribute the material to VAG TECH PRO users.
