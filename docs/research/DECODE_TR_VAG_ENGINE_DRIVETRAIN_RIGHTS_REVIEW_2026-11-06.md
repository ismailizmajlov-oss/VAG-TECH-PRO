# decode.tr VAG engine and drivetrain catalogue — rights and provenance review

Review date: 2026-11-06.

## Decision

**Quarantined; no download, automated extraction, offline caching, product display, redistribution or SQLite import.**

The public tool is useful as a discovery lead for VAG engine and drivetrain code research, but it is not an open-data source. Its publisher explicitly states that a source link is not an open licence or republication permission and that third-party documents remain subject to their owners' rights. No written reuse permission or dataset-specific licence was identified.

## What the public pages disclose

The engine-code page describes lookups by engine code and shows model and production-period context. The indexed page advertises 1,544 engine codes and 7,392 model/period records. The drivetrain section distinguishes transmission codes from differential codes; the indexed result advertises an Audi gearbox list of 2,383 codes and 3,867 applications. These counts are publisher/search-index claims, not independently reconciled against a downloadable snapshot.

The page also says its initial transmission dictionary is not a complete VAG transmission database. Its examples warn that a matching code alone does not establish gear ratios, ECU compatibility or mechanical fit. The service notes that some engine and drivetrain records derive from 2015 ETKA extracts; an empty end date must not be interpreted as current production.

## Publisher and rights statement

The About page identifies decode.tr as an independent research tool operated by Fatih Bayrak and disclaims official affiliation with Volkswagen Group and data providers. It states:

- some engine and drivetrain records derive from 2015 ETKA extracts;
- a source link is not an open licence or republication permission;
- third-party documents remain subject to their owners' rights.

The site footer states © 2026 All rights reserved. No dataset licence granting bulk reuse, local caching, third-party display or redistribution was found.

## Applicability limits

The visible model/period context is a research candidate only. It does not establish:
- exact vehicle variant or market;
- an exact engine/transmission pairing;
- ECU hardware/software applicability;
- DTC or test-plan applicability;
- repair procedures, wiring diagrams, pinouts, torque values, adaptations or service intervals;
- Russian or EAEU applicability.

The catalogue must not be merged with the VAG factory source layer or used to promote generic or community records into verified VAG-specific repair data.
