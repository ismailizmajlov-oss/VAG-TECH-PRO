## Namespace correction — 2026-11-06

A later standards-boundary audit found that the original 9,533-row projection included 174 `U3xxx` entries in a generic namespace. SAE J2012 identifies `U3xxx` as a mixed manufacturer-controlled / ISO/SAE-reserved family (section 5.7.4); the upstream snapshot does not provide the code-level evidence needed to treat every such row as generic. The corrected normalizer keeps the original source files and their hashes, quarantines all 174 `U3xxx` records in the generated review package, and imports only 9,359 records into `generic_dtc_research`. The raw upstream projection remains 9,533; PIDs remain 132. The correction is a namespace filter, not a claim that the remaining descriptions are independently validated or applicable to VAG vehicles. See [SAE J2012:2013, section 5.7.4](https://files.infocentre.io/files/docs_clients/126_2007751625_1263573_J2012_201303.pdf) and the current [SAE J2012 standard listing](https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/j2012_202511-diagnostic-trouble-code-definitions).
