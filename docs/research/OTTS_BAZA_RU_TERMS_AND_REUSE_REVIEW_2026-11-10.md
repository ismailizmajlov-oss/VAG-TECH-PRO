# OTTS-Baza.ru — current access terms and reuse boundary (2026-11-10)

## Decision

Keep the source restricted and discovery-only. Do not import public search cards or PDFs into VAG TECH PRO, and do not automate queries, create an account, subscribe, pay, cache, display or redistribute this source.

The current [access terms](https://otts-baza.ru/offer/) are dated 2026-10-14. They permit personal tasks and internal professional work with vehicles, but explicitly prohibit systematic extraction of a substantial part of the database for publication, resale or creation of a competing catalogue without a separate agreement. VAG TECH PRO is an offline repair-information product, so the ordinary internal-use allowance is not a licence to populate and redistribute a competing technical catalogue.

## Current observations

- The public [homepage](https://otts-baza.ru/) displayed 42,050 OTTS cards on 2026-11-10. Treat this only as a page counter, not a verified complete corpus count or a reuse permission.
- The site describes the material as EAEU OTTS registry data, but the claim was not independently verified against an official bulk dataset.
- Full PDF access requires a registered account and either a paid subscription or a limited free route. The terms allow one new available PDF every 24 hours after login and email confirmation.
- Paid access is limited to 25 new full PDFs per day and 600 per month per account under the reviewed terms.
- The [pricing page](https://otts-baza.ru/pricing/) returned HTTP 502 during this review. No current price is asserted or accepted.
- The terms say that commercial work by a user with vehicles is not itself prohibited. That does not override the separate prohibition on systematic extraction for a competing catalogue.

## Rights interpretation

No open licence was identified. The access terms are not a redistribution licence. They do not grant VAG TECH PRO permission for systematic metadata extraction, PDF harvesting, persistent SQLite caching, offline end-user display, third-party display or redistribution. A separate written agreement would have to expressly grant each of those rights before any acquisition.
