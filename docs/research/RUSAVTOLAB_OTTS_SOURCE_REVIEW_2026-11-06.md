# Rusavtolab OTTS index — source rights review (2026-11-06)

## Observation

- Landing page: https://otts.rusavtolab.ru/ claims 77,802 OTTS documents and says search and PDF downloads are free.
- About page: https://otts.rusavtolab.ru/o-servise/ identifies the service as a private service operated by SpecReg / RusAvtoLab, not as the state registry operator.
- Public offer: https://otts.rusavtolab.ru/oferta/ describes an electronic copy of an OTTS from open sources at 200 RUB per document. This conflicts with the landing-page free-download claim.

## Rights decision

No dataset-specific licence, written permission for bulk reuse, automated extraction, offline caching, third-party display or redistribution was identified. The public availability of a search form or a PDF copy does not grant those rights. No documents were downloaded and no source snapshot was created; therefore the source SHA-256 remains null.

## Applicability decision

The index is a document-discovery candidate only. No per-document upstream provenance export or reproducible bulk snapshot was established. No VAG-specific model/generation/year, engine/gearbox pairing, ECU, DTC or repair applicability is confirmed. The source remains permission_required, data_ingested=false, product_import_allowed=false.

## Product decision

Do not automate, scrape, cache, display or redistribute records until the operator provides written rights and a reproducible export with per-document source lineage. Do not pay for individual documents under this project without explicit owner approval.
