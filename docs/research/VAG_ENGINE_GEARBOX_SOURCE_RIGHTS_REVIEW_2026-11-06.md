# VAG engine and gearbox code source-rights review — 2026-11-06

## Decision

No new engine-code or gearbox-code records were imported. The sources below are useful for discovery, but the reviewed terms do not authorize the bulk extraction, local SQLite caching, display and redistribution required by VAG TECH PRO. Aggregate counts and publisher claims are recorded only as research metadata.

## Sam's VW World — gearbox code directory

- Directory: https://samworld.de/wissen/gkb/
- Terms: https://samworld.de/nutzbed.php
- The directory publicly claims 4,534 brand records, 3,489 unique gearbox codes, 565 codes shared by multiple brands and 701 records with gear-ratio data.
- Its terms (revision dated 23 September 2026) say that the site's own texts, photographs, graphics, compilations and other content may not be reproduced, published, edited or used commercially beyond statutory permissions without prior consent. Third-party rights remain with their respective owners.
- No dataset-specific open licence or permission for VAG TECH PRO was found.
- **Decision:** quarantined. No scraping, bulk extraction, copying, caching, bundling, display or redistribution. The aggregate counts are not evidence of exact fitment, Russian/EAEU market scope or engine/gearbox pairing.

## EngineCode.uk — Audi, Volkswagen, SEAT and Škoda engine-code directories

- Audi: https://www.enginecode.uk/audi
- Volkswagen: https://www.enginecode.uk/volkswagen
- SEAT: https://enginecode.uk/seat
- Škoda: https://www.enginecode.uk/skoda
- Terms: https://www.enginecode.uk/terms-and-conditions
- Editorial policy: https://www.enginecode.uk/editorial-policy
- The pages claim engine-code/specification/application coverage for UK and European vehicles and name manufacturer and EU sources. The review did not reconcile individual records against those primary sources.
- The terms state that text, images, database material and other content may be protected by copyright, database rights or other rights; where no licence or permission is stated, unrestricted republication or commercial reuse must not be assumed.
- The editorial policy says a cited source does not itself grant permission to reproduce images or text and distinguishes secondary evidence from direct manufacturer confirmation.
- **Decision:** candidate discovery only. No data import, automated extraction, offline cache, product display or redistribution without a specific licence or written permission and record-level source review.

## VAG-Wiki — existing quarantine reaffirmed

- Directory: https://vag-wiki.com/engines
- Terms: https://vag-wiki.com/terms-of-service
- The current terms prohibit unauthorized mass scraping, automated data extraction and redistribution without prior authorization; commercial scraping and AI training are restricted. The site states that technical values are compiled from mixed independent sources and disclaims guaranteed accuracy/currency.
- **Decision:** keep existing quarantine and do not import.

## Applicability and provenance rules

- A directory code is not proof that an engine or gearbox was installed in a particular vehicle.
- Do not infer a vehicle-generation/year or an engine/gearbox pair from aggregate counts or a broad model page.
- Any future permission must explicitly cover commercial use, bulk reuse, offline caching, display and redistribution, and must be linked to a dated evidence document with SHA-256.
- Any future imported record must preserve the exact source revision, market scope, model/generation/year evidence, applicability precision and verification status. Candidate data must remain separate from confirmed fitment.
