# OTTS.BOT — source-rights recheck (2026-11-10)

## Decision

Keep OTTS.BOT discovery-only and rights-restricted. Do not scrape, issue mass requests, download documents for VAG TECH PRO, cache the database, display its records as part of the offline product, or redistribute documents/database information. No account, subscription, payment, automated request, download, cache or import was used.

## Current primary evidence

- [Published User Agreement](https://www.otts.bot/terms), last updated 2026-08-26, rechecked 2026-11-10.
  - Clause 3 permits ordinary free search, but prohibits scraping/mass requests beyond ordinary user scenarios and prohibits resale, publication or other distribution of documents and database information as a proprietary information product.
  - Clauses 4.3–4.4 require contributors to warrant their own rights and grant the operator rights to place, reproduce, publicly display and deliver documents to the operator's users. This is not a sublicense to VAG TECH PRO.
  - Clause 5 reserves the site's presentation, database structure, section text and code to the operator. Rights to the underlying approval documents remain with their respective rightsholders.
- [OTTS search page](https://www.otts.bot/otts), rechecked 2026-11-10.
  - Free ordinary search is advertised.
  - The rendered result counter says 74,115, while the page headline says “46,000+”. The discrepancy remains unresolved; neither figure is an independently audited export.
  - One-off PDF delivery is advertised at 500 RUB per document. No document was purchased.
- [Homepage](https://www.otts.bot/), last independently observed 2026-11-09. The prior UI counters were 74,115 OTTS, 224,744 SBKTS and 1,655 OTSH; these remain provider UI observations only and were not re-audited in this recheck.

## Applicability boundary

The page exposes document-level search fields, but no exact VAG factory engine code, gearbox code, same-variant engine/gearbox pair, ECU mapping, DTC fitment, wiring, torque or repair applicability was established. The provider's technical-characteristics and engine sections were previously described as not yet populated.

Even if a specific document is later lawfully obtained for an individual user's own use, the published terms do not grant VAG TECH PRO a right to cache it in a redistributed offline product or display it to third parties. Underlying document rights must be checked separately.

## Reconsideration criteria

Reconsider only after separate written permission from the operator and relevant underlying document rightsholders expressly covers the intended bulk retrieval, persistent SQLite storage, offline caching, third-party desktop display, commercial use, updates and redistribution. The permission must identify the exact document population and distinguish document contents, database metadata and derivative indexes.

No request was sent, no paid terms were accepted, no account was created and no credentials were used.
