# Kazakhstan EOKNO TR TS 018/2011 public register review — 2026-11-07

## Decision

Register the EOKNO page as a **related Kazakhstan conformity-document discovery source only**. It is not currently a verified OTTS dataset and is not approved for product import.

The public page is titled “Реестр документов, подтверждающих соответствие требованиям ТР ТС 018/2011” and exposes a registration-number search field plus an EAEU/KZ scope filter. The title is broad, but it does not by itself prove that the register contains OTTS or OTSH records.

The official Kazakhstan State Revenue Committee press release dated 2023-05-12 specifically describes the EOKNO module for issuing SBKTS and the online publication of SBKTS records in the register. It does **not** confirm OTTS or OTSH coverage. Therefore the machine-readable manifest records SBKTS workflow coverage as confirmed, while OTTS and OTSH coverage remain unverified.

## Rights and data handling

- No dataset-specific open licence or written permission for bulk reuse, automated extraction, offline caching, third-party display or redistribution was identified.
- No documented API, bulk export contract, reproducible dataset snapshot or current dataset revision was identified.
- No records were downloaded. No source-file hash or snapshot SHA-256 is claimed.
- The source remains `permission_required`, `data_ingested=false`, and `product_import_allowed=false`.
- No VIN, applicant, address, owner or other personal data is to be collected.
- No VAG engine code, gearbox code, exact powertrain pair, ECU, DTC or repair applicability is established.

## Source evidence

1. [EOKNO public register](https://eokno.gov.kz/public-register/register-ktrm2.xhtml) — page title and public search controls observed on 2026-11-07.
2. [Kazakhstan State Revenue Committee press release, 2023-05-12](https://kgd.gov.kz/ru/news/press-reliz-po-avtomatizacii-v-edinoe-okno-po-eksportno-importnym-operaciyam-polucheniya) — confirms the SBKTS module and online publication of SBKTS records, not OTTS coverage.

## Re-entry criteria

Reconsider the source only after (a) the register's document-type coverage is verified from authoritative current documentation, including whether OTTS/OTSH records are present, and (b) the portal operator or data rightsholder provides an explicit licence or written permission covering the intended bulk retrieval, offline cache, display and redistribution. Any future data intake must be field-allowlisted, exclude personal identifiers, preserve original-file and row hashes, and keep document types separate.
