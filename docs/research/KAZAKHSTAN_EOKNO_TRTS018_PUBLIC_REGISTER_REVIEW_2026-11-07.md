# Kazakhstan EOKNO TR TS 018/2011 public register review — 2026-11-07

## Decision

Register the EOKNO page as a **related Kazakhstan conformity-document discovery source only**. It is an official OTTS/OTSH/SBKTS register workflow, but it is not approved for product import because bulk reuse and offline distribution rights remain unconfirmed.

The public page is titled “Реестр документов, подтверждающих соответствие требованиям ТР ТС 018/2011” and exposes a registration-number search field plus an EAEU/KZ scope filter. The title is broad, but it does not by itself prove that the register contains OTTS or OTSH records.

The official Kazakhstan State Revenue Committee release dated 2023-05-12 confirms the SBKTS module and publication of SBKTS records. A second official release from the Committee of Technical Regulation and Metrology dated 2023-12-28 states that after an OTTS/OTSH registration number is assigned, details are transferred online into the EOKNO conformity-document register and can be verified by QR or register search. OTTS/OTSH register workflow coverage is therefore confirmed as of 2023/2024. This does not establish the current completeness of the register or any reuse rights.

## Rights and data handling

- No dataset-specific open licence or written permission for bulk reuse, automated extraction, offline caching, third-party display or redistribution was identified.
- No documented API, bulk export contract, reproducible dataset snapshot or current dataset revision was identified.
- The latest indexed EOKNO homepage snapshot (reported as crawled approximately two months before 2026-11-07) lists the TR TS 018/2011 register and contains a notice saying new conformity-document issuance is handled by the KTRM Technical Regulation system at [techreg.gov.kz](https://techreg.gov.kz/index/); general EOKNO functions moved to KEDEN. A live homepage fetch timed out, so the notice's present-day status is not independently verified.
- No records were downloaded. No source-file hash or snapshot SHA-256 is claimed.
- The source remains `permission_required`, `data_ingested=false`, and `product_import_allowed=false`.
- No VIN, applicant, address, owner or other personal data is to be collected.
- No VAG engine code, gearbox code, exact powertrain pair, ECU, DTC or repair applicability is established.

## Re-entry criteria

Reconsider product import only after (a) the current register's operational status and record completeness are verified and (b) the portal operator or data rightsholder provides an explicit licence or written permission covering the intended bulk retrieval, offline cache, display and redistribution. Any future data intake must be field-allowlisted, exclude personal identifiers, preserve original-file and row hashes, and keep document types separate.
